              Before you set out

              Tokyo to Cairo in 50 days

              My daughter Holy is dying, and it's DIO's fault: the vampire who stole my grandfather's body has risen after a century under the sea, and his return woke a Stand, a fighting spirit, inside her that her gentle heart can't control. Avdol and I give her fifty days, so my grandson Jotaro and I are racing across the world to DIO's lair in Cairo to finish him first. This is my journal of the trip — and yes, I'm the hero of it.

              Travel the route one stop at a time. At each stop, your journal records what's on the menu there and the story behind it, the real city, and the tarot card or Egyptian god behind each Stand fought there.

              At each stop, the Stand encounters wait face down: turn the cards over in order to reveal them and collect their stamps. Some fights happen on the way: an ambush stops you partway along the route, and Continue finishes the trip. Press Next destination to move on and advance the day counter. Select any stop you've already reached on the map to look back at it, or any collected stamp to see its card again.

              Day 50 of 50

              Journey's end: Cairo

              On the fiftieth day we found DIO, and he could stop time itself; Kakyoin spent his last breath showing us how. DIO drained my blood, but Jotaro learned to stop time too, shattered him for good, and gave my blood back — so I'm writing this, more or less alive. Avdol, Iggy and Kakyoin didn't come home, and Polnareff, Jotaro and I said goodbye with heavy hearts. Back in Japan, Holy has woken up and is waiting for us; this journal is for the three friends who brought her back.
